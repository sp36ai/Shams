Your privacy matters. Shams al-Asrār does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Data is collected only to provide the service, and you may request deletion at any time.

1. Who We Are

Shams al-Asrār is a horary astrology application developed and operated by Astro Sarfaraz (sole proprietor), based in India. The app is available on the Google Play Store under the package name com.astrosarfaraz.shamsalasrar.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — email address or phone number provided during sign-up via Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In or email/password).
  • Location data — if you grant location permission, the app reads your device's location and keeps it on your device only, to show local sky details on the home screen (such as the planetary hour). It is not sent to our servers or to OpenAI, it is not used to calculate your readings, and it is not used for tracking. Without permission, the home screen uses a default location instead.
  • Question content — the text of questions you submit to the oracle, stored in your personal reading history in Firestore.
  • Names (optional) — your name and your mother's name, only if you choose to enter them, so the oracle can address you personally. They are stored on your device and sent with each question; we do not store them on our servers.
  • Onboarding answers — your answers to three short questions when you first open the app, used once to choose the tone of your readings. They are not stored on our servers.
  • Follow-up messages — messages you write about a reading. They are kept in the reading's history on your device and are not stored on our servers.
  • Usage data — question count, daily quota consumption, and subscription status, stored to enforce plan limits.
  • Purchase information — subscription plan, purchase token, and expiry date from Google Play Billing, used to verify and activate your subscription.
  • Device information — anonymised device identifiers used by Firebase for App Check (fraud prevention). We do not collect your full device ID.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — if the app crashes or encounters an error, Firebase Crashlytics records a crash report containing the device model, operating system version, app version, and a technical stack trace. Crash reports do not include your questions, readings, or account details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your personalised oracle reading using the astronomical engine.
  • To write your reading and replies in words, using OpenAI (see Section 4).
  • To maintain your reading history within the app.
  • To verify subscription purchases and enforce plan quotas.
  • To authenticate your account securely.
  • To prevent abuse and protect the integrity of the service.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix technical problems in the app.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — Authentication, Firestore database, and Cloud Functions. Data is stored in the asia-south1 (Mumbai) region. Firebase Privacy
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC) — In-app subscription processing. We receive a purchase token and product ID; full payment details are handled by Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) — Crash reporting and stability monitoring. Crash reports are retained by Crashlytics for 90 days. Firebase Privacy
  • OpenAI (OpenAI API) — Your reading itself (the verdict, its timing and any recommended practice) is calculated by our own astrology engine, not by OpenAI. We use OpenAI's language models only to put that result into words: the text of your reading, the oracle's replies to your follow-up messages, a check that your question is one the oracle can answer, and the choice of tone for your readings from your onboarding answers. For this purpose we send OpenAI: the text of your question; the follow-up messages you write about a reading; the result our engine calculated for that reading (with follow-up messages, this may include the date and time, in UTC, that the reading was cast); when you compare readings, the questions and results of the readings being compared; your name and your mother's name, only if you have entered them in the app; and your answers to the three onboarding questions.

    We do not send OpenAI your email address, phone number, account ID, payment details, device identifiers, GPS coordinates, precise device location or time zone.

    OpenAI does not use API inputs and outputs to train its models by default, unless the API account explicitly opts in to data sharing. We configure requests not to store Responses API response state. However, OpenAI may retain API content in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, or longer where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect its services or others from harm. These retention practices are governed by OpenAI's applicable API data policies. OpenAI may process this data outside India, including in the United States. OpenAI Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage and Retention

Your account data and reading history are stored in Firebase Firestore (Mumbai region, India). Local device preferences are stored on-device using encrypted storage and are never transmitted to our servers.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Deleting your account (in-app, under Settings → Account → Delete Account) permanently and immediately deletes your profile, every reading, your quota record, and your subscription/plan status, and deletes your account credentials entirely — this is not a manual or delayed process on our end.

Retained after account deletion: a limited set of fraud-prevention and security records — the internal binding between a redeemed subscription purchase token and the account that redeemed it (so a leaked purchase token cannot later be redeemed a second time), and abuse/security event logs. These records do not contain your name, email, question text, or reading content — question text is one-way hashed before being logged, and reading content is never included — and are retained only for fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance purposes, not as personal data about you.

  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data.

  • Withdrawal of consent — you may revoke location permission at any time via your device settings; the home screen's sky details will then use a default location. You may also remove your optional names at any time in the app's Settings.
