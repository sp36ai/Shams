Data & Account Deletion  ·  Package: com.astrosarfaraz.shamsalasrar

This page explains how to delete your Shams al-Asrār account and the personal data associated with it, in line with Google Play's User Data policy.

Delete your account from inside the app

The fastest way is directly in the app:

  1. Open Shams al-Asrār and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and personal data are removed immediately.

Request deletion without the app

If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion using the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing. Send the email address you signed up with and ask for your account and data to be deleted. We action verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your sign-in account (email / Google identity).
  • Every saved reading and its question text.
  • Your usage quota and free-trial records.
  • Your locally stored profile (your name, mother's name, and cached location) on the device.

Retained for a limited time. For fraud prevention and to meet financial record-keeping obligations, we keep a minimal transaction and abuse trail that is tied only to an opaque account identifier — never your name, email, question text, or location. This contains no readable personal content and is not used to re-identify you.

Purchases

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — manage or cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.
