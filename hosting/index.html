Sun of Secrets — a horary oracle, not a horoscope

Built by Astro Sarfaraz

Shams al-Asrār is a horary oracle app: celestial counsel descended from centuries of classical Islamic astrological tradition. It is not a horoscope and not a personality test. Every question a seeker asks is anchored to the exact moment it is asked — precise to the second — and the app reads that moment: the sun's position, the stars' arrangement, and what the advancing light of that instant illuminates about the seeker's situation.

Five celestial powers converge at the instant of every question — the cosmic validators. When these forces align, the answer is certain. When they scatter, the answer is more complex — but it is still true.

The Digital Watch Oracle

Every answer is anchored to the seeker's precise timestamp — the exact moment the question is asked. No location or birth details required.

Shams al-Asrār is available on Android.
