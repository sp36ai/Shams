# Shams al-Asrār — RKP Rule Confirmation Sheet

Prepared for: Astro Sarfaraz

Purpose: confirm the rules the watch engine should follow, before any disputed
logic is changed. This is not a request to compare RKP with classical Vedic
astrology. We want the rule **you** teach. If a rule is not yours, or you have
not decided it, please say UNKNOWN — we will leave the code as it is.

For each item below, tick one: **Confirmed as implemented / Correct it / UNKNOWN**.
Where you correct it, please give the rule as an if-then statement and, if
possible, one example chart. Where you can, name the document or lesson it comes
from; otherwise "my own teaching" is a valid answer.

The "Now" line says what the app does today. The sample charts are in
`case_records_blind.md`.

## Part 1 — The frame (please confirm first; everything depends on it)

**1.1 Watch minute → 1st Ghar.** Now: minutes 00–04 give Hamal as the 1st Ghar,
05–09 Saur, 10–14 Jauza, 15–19 Sartan, 20–24 Asad, 25–29 Sunbula, 30–34
Meezan, 35–39 Aqrab, 40–44 Qaus, 45–49 Jadi, 50–54 Dalw, 55–59 Hoot. The other
signs follow in order as Ghars 2–12. Only the minute is used.
Is this exactly your rule? Which clock minute counts (the moment the question is
sent, or when it is read)?

**1.2 Planets.** Now: real positions at the moment of asking (Lahiri, sidereal),
placed in whichever Ghar their sign falls in. Rahu and Dhanab use the mean node.
Confirm, or correct (true node?).

## Part 2 — How a verdict is reached

**2.1 Numbers or judgment?** Now: the app adds points and reads the total
(for example −5 or less means "No", 5 or more means "Yes"). Does RKP use points
at all? If yes, what does each factor score and what totals give which answer?
If RKP is judged by order of strength instead, please describe the order.

**2.2 Benefic and malefic.** Now: only Mushtari and Zuhrah are benefic; only
Zuhal, Mirrikh, Ras and Dhanab are malefic; Shams, Qamar and Utarid count as
neither. Which planets are benefic or malefic? Does that change with the
question, the sign, the Ghar, or the planet's condition (for example Qamar's
phase, or Utarid when joined to a malefic)?

**2.3 Equal weight.** Now: every benefic is +2 and every malefic −2, so four
malefics and two benefics mean a malefic lands on a Ghar more often. Does one
strong benefic cancel a malefic? Is a malefic stronger than a benefic? What
decides?

**2.4 The 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th Ghars.** Now: a malefic there is penalised
like anywhere else. Can a malefic in these Ghars help the matter? If yes: for
which questions, for sitting only or also for aspecting, and does it cancel a
bad sign or only soften it?

**2.5 Zuhal and work.** Now: Zuhal on or aspecting the 10th Ghar is treated as
an obstruction and forces "Delayed" or "No". For a job or work question, what
does Zuhal on or aspecting the 10th mean — support, delay, blockage, steady
effort? Does it depend on Zuhal's sign or on other planets?

**2.6 Rahu and Dhanab.** Now: they aspect the 5th, 7th and 9th from where they
sit, like Mushtari. Do they cast aspects in RKP? If yes, which? If no, do they
act only where they sit?

**2.7 Friendly signs.** Now: a planet in its own or exalted sign counts as
strong, in an enemy or debilitated sign as weak, and in a friendly or neutral
sign as nothing either way. Should a friendly sign count for the matter?

**2.8 Planetary friendships.** Now: one fixed table, one-way (Utarid counts
Qamar an enemy, but Qamar counts Utarid a friend). Is this table yours? Is the
friendship permanent, or does the chart change it?

**2.9 Retrograde and combust.** Now: if the ruler of the matter is retrograde the
answer becomes "Reversing"; if it is combust, one point is lost. What do you
teach for each? What distance from Shams counts as combust, and does it differ
by planet?

**2.10 The 11th Ghar.** Now: the 11th is always the Ghar of fulfilment, for
every question. Is that so, or does it depend on the question?

**2.11 Strength by sign.** Now: exaltation Shams-Hamal, Qamar-Saur,
Mirrikh-Jadi, Utarid-Sunbula, Mushtari-Sartan, Zuhrah-Hoot, Zuhal-Meezan;
debilitation is the opposite sign; own signs Shams Asad, Qamar Sartan, Mirrikh
Hamal and Aqrab, Utarid Jauza and Sunbula, Mushtari Qaus and Hoot, Zuhrah Saur
and Meezan, Zuhal Jadi and Dalw. Ras is exalted in Saur and Dhanab in Aqrab,
and neither has an own sign. Confirm or correct each (the node exaltations
especially).

**2.12 Confidence (low priority).** Now: how sure the answer sounds depends
only on the size of the total score, and drops one level when a benefic and a
malefic both touch the matter. Is that how you want confidence shown?
