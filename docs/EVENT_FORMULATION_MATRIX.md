## Overview: The Shams Method Philosophy

In the **Shams Method**, multi-faceted events are governed by:

- **Primary Anchors ($P$):** The dominant house(s) signified by the Cusp Sub-Lord (CSL)
- **Reinforcing Chains ($S$):** Secondary houses that amplify or contextualize the Primary Anchor
- **Systemic Negations ($N = P - 1$ or event-specific terminal vectors):** Houses that directly oppose, weaken, or deny the Primary Anchor

**Core Principle:** Single-house analysis is insufficient for complex life inquiries. The engine must evaluate **compound vectors** across **multiple cuspal sub-lords** to render judgment on compound events—litigation outcomes, medical interventions, financial crises, and competitive selection processes.

### I. Litigation & Conflict Resolution

**Conceptual Anchor:** Litigation is a zero-sum confrontation between the **Native ($1^{\text{st}}$ House)** and the **Opponent ($7^{\text{th}}$ House)**.

#### The Victory Vector ($6 - 11$)

**Components:**

- **The $6^{\text{th}}$ house:** Represents the defeat of the open enemy ($12^{\text{th}}$ from the $7^{\text{th}}$ house).
- **The $11^{\text{th}}$ house:** Represents the final fulfillment of desire and favorable decree.

**Rule:**

> If the **$6^{\text{th}}$ CSL** resides in the star of a planet signifying **$6$ or $11$**, victory is assured.
> If it simultaneously links to the $1^{\text{st}}$ or $10^{\text{th}}$, the judgment reinforces personal status and legal vindication.

**Judgment Conditions:**

1. **$6^{\text{th}}$ CSL Star Lord** must signify $6, 1,$ or $11$ at Level A.
2. **$11^{\text{th}}$ CSL** must not be compromised by $5, 7, 8,$ or $12$.
3. If Mars or Sun occupies the $6^{\text{th}}$ cusp or its Star, combat victory (or firm legal dominance) is guaranteed.

**Sub-rule: Status Reinforcement**

- If the $10^{\text{th}}$ CSL also signifies $6$, the verdict elevates the native's professional or legal standing.

---

#### The Defeat / Penalization Vector ($12 - 5 - 8$)

**Components:**

- **The $12^{\text{th}}$ house:** Represents personal loss, imprisonment, or legal penalties ($6^{\text{th}}$ from the $7^{\text{th}}$ house—opponent's victory).
- **The $5^{\text{th}}$ house:** Negates the native's $6^{\text{th}}$ house ($12^{\text{th}}$ to $6$)—removes agency or decision-making authority.
- **The $8^{\text{th}}$ house:** Represents humiliation, mental agony, and financial damages awarded against the native.

**Rule:**

> If the **$6^{\text{th}}$ CSL** links predominantly to **$7, 8,$ and $12$**, the opponent dominates the dispute, leading to unfavorable verdicts or costly settlements.

**Judgment Conditions:**

1. **$6^{\text{th}}$ CSL Sub-Lord** must signify $12, 8,$ or $7$ at Level A or Level B.
2. **$11^{\text{th}}$ CSL** must signify $12$ or $8$ (negation of gains).
3. If Saturn or Rahu occupies the $8^{\text{th}}$ cusp, prolonged suffering and appeals are probable.

**Sub-rule: Financial Drain**

- If the $2^{\text{nd}}$ CSL signifies $8$ or $12$, legal costs exceed recovery.

---

#### The Compromise Vector ($5 - 9$)

**Components:**

- **The $5^{\text{th}}$ house:** Releases/negates the $6^{\text{th}}$ ($12^{\text{th}}$ from $6$)—ends active conflict.
- **The $9^{\text{th}}$ house:** Represents judicial mediation, legal arbitration, and settlement authority.

**Rule:**

> If the **$6^{\text{th}}$ CSL** links to the **$5^{\text{th}}$ and $9^{\text{th}}$** without activating $8$ or $12$, both parties reach an out-of-court mutual settlement.

**Judgment Conditions:**

1. **$6^{\text{th}}$ CSL Star Lord** must signify $5$ and $9$ simultaneously.
2. **$11^{\text{th}}$ CSL** must not signify $8$ or $12$.
3. Venus or Jupiter occupying $9^{\text{th}}$ cusp accelerates settlement.

**Sub-rule: Satisfaction Degree**

- If the $2^{\text{nd}}$ CSL also signifies $11$, the settlement amount is favorable.

### Pseudo-Code Framework for Multi-Vector Evaluation

```python
def evaluate_shams_event(native_chart, question_query, operative_period):
    """
    Evaluate a complex Shams Method event across multi-house vectors.

    Args:
        native_chart: SiderealChart with Lahiri ayanamsa and Placidus houses
        question_query: User's inquiry text and category (litigation, exam, health, etc.)
        operative_period: Current Dasha/Bhukti/Antara period

    Returns:
        EventJudgment(promise: bool, verdict: str, confidence: float, timeline: Period)
    """

    event_type = classify_event(question_query)

    # Extract Cusp Sub-Lords for all relevant houses
    csls = {}
    for house_num in HOUSE_MATRIX[event_type]['primary'] + HOUSE_MATRIX[event_type]['secondary']:
        cusp_degree = native_chart.get_cusp_longitude(house_num)
        csls[house_num] = native_chart.get_cusp_sub_lord(cusp_degree)

    # Get Star Lords and Sub-Lords for each CSL
    star_lords = {h: get_star_lord(csls[h]) for h in csls}
    sub_lords = {h: get_sub_lord(csls[h]) for h in csls}

    # Extract Level A-D significations for each lord
    star_significations = {h: get_signified_houses(star_lords[h], level='A') for h in star_lords}
    sub_significations = {h: get_signified_houses(sub_lords[h], level='A') for h in sub_lords}

    # Route to specific event evaluator
    if event_type == "LITIGATION_VICTORY":
        return evaluate_litigation(csls, star_significations, sub_significations)

    elif event_type == "LITIGATION_SETTLEMENT":
        return evaluate_compromise(csls, star_significations, sub_significations)

    elif event_type == "HEALTH_DISEASE":
        return evaluate_disease(csls, star_significations, sub_significations)

## 6. DBA Window Establishment & Transit-Based Timing

4. **Phase 4:** Pinpoint exact date via celestial transits

#### Phase 1: Establish the Active DBA Window

**Principle:** A promised event only manifests when the current Dasha/Bhukti/Antara lords are **strong significators of the event houses**.

Assume the native runs the following planetary periods:

- **Maha Dasha (Major Period):** Moon (Star Lord of 5th CSL; signifies 8, 11)

```

  ✓ Moon → 8, 11 (present in event houses)
  ✓ Mercury → 2, 11 (present in event houses)

- If all three DBA lords (Dasha, Bhukti, Antara) signify event houses → **Event imminent (0–60 days)**

## 8. Retrograde (Vakra) Planet Logic

### Overview: Temporal Circuit Breakers

In the Shams Method, retrograde planets act as **temporal circuit breakers** within the deterministic engine. Their behavior is not a loss of strength, but rather an **alteration in directionality and timing**.

The system treats retrograde motion strictly according to where the planet sits in the stellar hierarchy (CSL, Star Lord, or Sub Lord) and the nature of the event being queried.

---

### Positional Impact of Retrograde Planets

#### A. Retrograde Cuspal Sub-Lord (The Anchor)

When the primary CSL of the queried house is retrograde, the native's initial efforts toward the event will face **reversals, revisions, or false starts**.

**Engine Verdict:** `EVENT_REQUIRES_RESTART`

- The event is not denied, but it requires repeated effort
- The native will likely abandon the pursuit temporarily and return to it later
- Expected retries: 2–3 attempts before success
- Timeline shift: Add 3–6 months to expected manifestation

**Example:** If the 5th CSL (speculation/windfall) is retrograde, the native might buy a lottery ticket, then abandon the plan, then return to it weeks later when the CSL becomes direct.

#### B. Retrograde Star Lord (The Result)

The Star Lord dictates the result. If the Star Lord is retrograde but signifies favorable houses, the promise is **mathematically valid but temporarily suspended in time**.

**Engine Verdict:** `EVENT_DELAYED`

- The event cannot manifest while the Star Lord is retrograde in transit
- It remains in a holding pattern and will execute only after the planet assumes direct motion
- Calculate direct motion date using ephemeris
- New execution window: Star Lord's retrograde end date + 2–3 weeks

**Example:** If Moon (Star Lord of windfall) is retrograde, the promised gain is real but must wait until the Moon turns direct.

#### C. Retrograde Sub Lord (The Final Verdict)

This is the **most critical failure point** in the Shams Method. The Sub Lord is the ultimate gatekeeper; if it is retrograde, it **withdraws its consent**.

**Engine Verdict:** `EVENT_DENIED`

- Even if the Star Lord promises a spectacular windfall or perfect marriage, a retrograde Sub Lord aborts the event at the eleventh hour
- The promise dissolves before materialization
- No rescheduling possible (unless event type triggers Reversal Override)

**Corollary:** If a direct Sub Lord is placed in the Star of a retrograde planet, the outcome is similarly **destabilized** → `EVENT_UNCERTAIN`

**Example:** Jupiter (Sub Lord) retrograde → Even with Moon (Star Lord) promising a windfall, Jupiter's retrograde veto blocks it entirely.

---

### The Reversal Override (When Retrograde is Favorable)

The deterministic engine does **not** apply the `EVENT_DENIED` logic universally. Retrograde literally means "moving backward." Therefore, for queries where the native **desires a return to a previous state**, retrograde significators become **mandatory for success**.

- Identify the currently active Bhukti (sub-period) lord
- Identify the currently active Antara (sub-sub-period) lord
- Store: `DBA_Array = [dasha_lord, bhukti_lord, antara_lord]`

#### Step 3.2: RP Array Formulation

- Capture the 5 Ruling Planets at the exact moment the query was cast
- Lagna Star Lord, Lagna Sign Lord, Moon Star Lord, Moon Sign Lord, Day Lord
- Store: `RP_Array = [lagna_star, lagna_sign, moon_star, moon_sign, day_lord]`

#### Step 3.3: The Intersection Filter

- Cross-reference the arrays: `Operative = DBA_Array ∩ RP_Array`
- Only planets in **both** arrays can trigger the event
- Store: `Operative_Significators = [planets in intersection]`

#### Step 3.4: Stigmatization Scrub

Remove any intersected planet that:

- Is **Combust (Astangata)** (within ~8° of Sun)
- Whose Sub Lord signifies the negating house ($N$)
- Is in mutual retrograde conflict with another operative planet

#### Step 3.5: Transit Lock

Project the remaining operative planets into the ephemeris:

1. **Level 1 (Month):** Find when Sun transits a Nakshatra owned by operative planet → 1–2 day range
2. **Level 2 (Day):** Find when Moon transits a Nakshatra owned by operative planet → hour/minute precision
3. **Level 3 (Moment):** Find when Lagna crosses the Sub of operative planet → second precision

Store: `Execution_Timestamp = [calculated exact date/time]`
